ØG AI Inference Energy & Carbon Transparency Plan
dAIOS + Phala TEE Verification + Blockchain Aggregation
1. Executive Summary
The AI industry runs on black box infrastructure — no visibility into how much energy each AI request consumes, no cryptographic proof of the claims, and no public audit trail.ØG’s decentralized AI operating system (dAIOS) can lead the industry by becoming the first AI platform to deliver verifiable, per-inference energy data — an achievement no centralized AI provider can match.This is done by:1. Capturing real-time energy usage for every inference across the network2. Cryptographically verifying these numbers via Phala’s GPU Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) for participating providers3. Aggregating results on-chain and making them public via climate.0g.ai
2. Why This Matters
For AI users and developers:- Gives trustworthy sustainability metrics at the model and provider level- Enables choosing providers based on energy efficiency & transparencyFor ØG’s competitive position:- First to market with provable sustainability claims- Leverages decentralization + cryptographic verification as a unique selling point- Creates network effects as more providers opt into verification to earn the 'Verified' badge
3. Problem with Traditional AI Providers
| Challenge | Centralized AI (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) | ØG Opportunity ||-----------|--------------------------------------------|----------------|| Per-inference energy transparency | ❌ None | ✅ Network-wide || Cryptographic proof of claims | ❌ None | ✅ Via Phala TEE || Public audit trail | ❌ None | ✅ On-chain from genesis || Model/provider-specific accuracy | ❌ None | ✅ Yes || Sustainability story | ❌ Marketing only | ✅ Measured + verified |
4. ØG Advantage
- Decentralized Serving Layer — Every provider can report energy usage in real time.- Phala Integration — GPU TEE verification for providers who opt in, with hardware-attested results.- Blockchain Aggregation — Immutable historical tracking by wallet, model, and provider.- Public Transparency — climate.0g.ai displays verified & efficient providers, historical trends, and wallet-level carbon footprints.
5. Implementation Plan
Phase 1 — Real-Time Energy Measurement (Network-Wide)- All service providers return energy usage via:  - x-gpu-wh: <float> (preferred), or  - x-gpu-avg-watts: <float> + x-gpu-active-ms: <int>- Broker middleware reads and labels:  - Verified (Phala TEE) — Attested, hardware-measured  - Reported — Self-reported without attestation  - Estimated — Derived from model profile & token count- Results are passed to ØG chain for aggregation.Phase 2 — Phala TEE Verification (Provider Opt-in, High Trust)- Providers run inference in Phala GPU TEEs (e.g., H100/H200).- Each inference request includes RA report with:  - Model ID/version  - Input/output hashes  - Measured Wh- Broker verifies attestation → marks 'Verified (Phala TEE)' in climate.0g.ai.Phase 3 — On-Chain Aggregation & Public Dashboard- ØG chain stores aggregated data by wallet, provider, and model.- climate.0g.ai displays:  - Historical energy & carbon footprint from genesis  - Rankings of verified, efficient providers  - Wallet-level usage impact
